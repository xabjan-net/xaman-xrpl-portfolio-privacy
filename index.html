Privacy Policy

Xaman — XRPL Portfolio

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Xaman — XRPL Portfolio is a browser extension designed to provide users with a convenient dashboard for viewing and monitoring information related to their XRP Ledger (XRPL) accounts and digital asset portfolios.

This Privacy Policy describes the information accessed by the extension and how that information is used, stored, and protected.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the features being used, the extension may access the following information:

  • Public XRPL account addresses.
  • Public XRP account balances.
  • Publicly available digital asset information.
  • Public blockchain transaction records.
  • Basic extension preferences and configuration settings.

The extension does not request users' wallet recovery phrases, private keys, or wallet passwords.

3. Purpose of Information Use

Information accessed by the extension is used exclusively to provide its portfolio-related functionality, including:

  • Displaying XRPL portfolio information.
  • Monitoring XRP balances.
  • Presenting available asset information.
  • Retrieving publicly available transaction activity.
  • Maintaining user interface preferences.

4. Data Storage

The extension may use Chrome's local storage functionality to save basic preferences and settings required for its operation.

Public blockchain information is retrieved through supported XRPL network endpoints or infrastructure providers.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

The extension does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

To retrieve blockchain information, the extension may communicate with XRPL network endpoints or third-party API providers. Such requests may include public account addresses and technical connection information necessary to process the requested data.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Advertising and Tracking

The extension does not use portfolio information for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking. It does not create advertising profiles based on users' account activity.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the extension.

The extension does not intentionally store or transmit users' private keys, recovery phrases, or wallet passwords.

8. User Control and Data Removal

Users can remove locally stored extension preferences and settings by clearing the extension's stored data or uninstalling the extension.

Public blockchain information is maintained by the XRP Ledger network and cannot be modified or deleted by the extension.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored preferences remain available until they are changed or removed by the user. Public blockchain information is retrieved from the XRPL network and is not controlled by the extension.

10. Children's Privacy

The extension is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the extension's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page.
